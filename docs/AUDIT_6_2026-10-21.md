# AUDIT 6 — The Repo Against the Datasheets

Written 2026-10-21 15:30 UTC in Claude Code by anthropic/claude-opus-5-5 (dimension reviews by
anthropic/claude-fable-5-1 and anthropic/claude-opus-5-5 subagents)

Audits 1–5 checked this repository against itself: does the prose agree with `spec_values.json`, does
the netlist agree with the BOM. They did that well. This audit asks a different question: **does the
repository agree with the parts it names, with physics, and with the regulators?** Every external claim
below cites a vendor datasheet, kernel source or official page. Anything not confirmed at the source is
marked UNVERIFIED.

Baseline: `main` @ `19a789c`, `python3 scripts/verify_all.py` → 16/16 PASS. That result is correct and
is beside the point: a wrong number in `spec_values.json` passes every phase, because every phase
compares against it.

**Headline.** The architecture has three fundamental errors that no amount of prose fixes. They need
decisions, not edits:

1. **The charger cannot charge the battery chemistry the product is built around.**
2. **The CM4 has no low-power state that keeps RAM alive**, so the "dual-brain, session hot in RAM,
   15 mW, <0.3 s" pillar does not exist on this silicon.
3. **The named panels, the named cell and the chassis do not fit together.** The real 10.3" panel is
   wider on its short axis than the 170 mm outer chassis. The 32 Wh LiFePO4 cell needs an energy
   density that the vendor LFP pouch data does not reach.

The electrical design artifacts (schematic, netlist, PCB, gerbers, CPL) have no connectivity. They are
script-written placeholders, and the netlist contains pin assignments that would short the battery if
built. The previous stage estimate of "architecture + narrative complete, engineering ~5% complete"
(AUDIT 1) still stands.

**Owner decisions taken after this audit (2026-10-21):**
- **ADR-031: CM5 is the compute standard** (CM5116064). X4 still applies: the CM5's power states
  match the CM4's. D3 below narrows to "the RP2350 owns the instant modes; Linux resumes from off or
  hibernate", and the RK3566 / i.MX93 options are withdrawn.
- **ADR-032: Kaleido 4 is the only panel, and V1 waits for its release.** X5 (panel outline), H6
  (IT8951 speed) and H8 (panel facts) become "unknown until the E Ink datasheet"; D4 is decided.
  The IT8951 module is the bench stand-in only.

Stage, in hardware-team terms: **pre-EVT concept**. The right next step is a dev-kit bench (§8), not a
board spin.

## 0. What SolarDeck is (as understood by this audit)

A 10.3" e-paper slate: a Raspberry Pi CM4 (Linux, Sway) plus an always-on RP2350 co-processor that runs
the clock/reader/radio while Linux is off; an IT8951 EPDC over SPI; LiFePO4 1S pack; a small bezel
solar strip; LTE, LoRa, NFC, Wacom EMR; IP67 target; external keyboard. The repo is ~16k lines, about
85% prose, a strong single-source-of-truth discipline (`spec_values.json` + Phase 10), and a good
ADR log. The intent and the process are sound. The part choices behind the three headline pillars are
not.

## 2. High — wrong today, fixable without a pillar decision

**Power and energy**
- **H1 Indoor solar is ~50× short.** BOM part SM141K06L is mono c-Si (184 mW STC, 9.66 cm²), not
  "a-Si". At 500 lux c-Si yields ~40–80 µW after BQ25504 — below even the 110 µW clock claim and ~50×
  below the 4.5 mW idle that Phase 14 compares against. Phase 14's "4.2×" uses outdoor insolation
  (4.28 kWh/m²/day), so it proves the outdoor case and nothing about 500 lux. Also BQ25504 VIN(DC) max
  is 3 V and the cell's Vmpp is 3.35 V / Voc 4.15 V; cold start is 600 mV, not 330 mV.
- **H2 Reverse boost overloads the cell path.** 15.8 W OTG (and the 17.5 W 5 V rail) at 3.0–3.3 V and
  ~90% efficiency is 5.2–6.5 A from one cell, above BQ25895 ISYS abs-max 5 A and above the protector's
  ~4.2 A OCD. Cap OTG near 2 A or re-architect.
- **H3 NTC network.** NCP18XH103F03RB is B = 3380 K, not 3950 K. With 5.23k/30.1k and BQ25895
  thresholds: cold lockout ≈ 0.7 °C, charging stops ≈ 45 °C. The 45–60 °C taper zone does not exist.
- **H4 TPS62840 is a buck**, not buck-boost; from LiFePO4 the "3.3 V" always-on rail is VBAT minus
  dropout for nearly the whole discharge.
- **H5 Energy arithmetic.** 32 Wh ÷ 4.5 mW = 296 d (241 d at 26 Wh), not "300+"; MVP tiers back out to
  a 35 Wh pack (banned value); README 16–20 h at 1.6–2.2 W is 14.5–20 h; "24–32 h coding",
  "10.5–11 h workhorse" and "35–42 h reading" match no tier. Reader tier ignores IT8951 standby
  (Waveshare module: ~0.1 W standby) — with the EPDC powered the reader lasts ~13 days, not ~300.

**Compute, display, firmware**
- **H6 IT8951 is 24 MHz SPI max and has no 1 bpp host format** (2/3/4/8 bpp). A full 2 bpp frame is
  219 ms at 24 MHz; with an A2 waveform ≈ 3 fps. `epdc_spi_mhz: 32`, "82 ms", "6 fps" and the DTS
  `spi-max-frequency = <32000000>` are all wrong. (IT8951 datasheet §9.3, §7.5.1.)
- **H7 Keystroke "18–20 ms" and pen "15–20 ms" are 6–20× below waveform physics.** Mode table:
  A2 120 ms, DU 260 ms, GC16 450 ms. `refreshd.h` ranks DU (≈12 ms) faster than A2, which is
  inverted, and `test_refreshd.c:80-84` locks that inversion in. Spec's own key
  `partial_rect_latency_ms` is 30, also unreachable on the waveform alone.
- **H8 Panel SKU facts.** 1872×1404 at 10.3" is a Carta panel; 10.3" Kaleido 3 is 2480×1860
  (300/150 PPI). `ppi: 227` with `colour_ppi: 150` cannot be one panel. IT8951 has no colour-filter
  processing; Kaleido 4 needs a different TCON (ADR-025 itself) and is not buyable bare.
- **H9 Thermal "viscosity assist" contradicts itself.** The CM4 is asleep in the product's own model,
  a static page draws 0 W, holding +10 K on 0.033 m² needs ~3–4 W, and HARDWARE_SPEC §4.3 says the
  clamp puts "zero heat" into the panel. Keep temperature-indexed LUTs; delete the heating claim.
- **H10 Wake-word cascade.** SPH0645LM4H-B is a plain I²S mic with no VAD. RP2350 at 150 MHz is
  ~39–51 mW (datasheet Table 1693), not ~14 mW; TFLM/microWakeWord has no RP2350 port; Resemblyzer
  (PyTorch) cannot run on a Cortex-M33.
- **H11 DTS will not work.** MAX98357A is declared clock master (it has no clock outputs);
  `sdmode-gpios` on GPIO18 = PCM_CLK; no `ite,it8951` binding exists anywhere; HRDY is GPIO27 in DTS
  vs GPIO24 in spec (GPIO24 is the RF switch in DTS); charger/INA3221 on the CM4 bus in DTS but the RP
  bus in the netlist.
- **H12 Firmware does not drive hardware.** `main.c` never writes the TMUX/5 V/WAKE/PWRFAIL outputs
  after transitions; the solar ADC ignores the 10:1 divider; `PIN_SOLAR_ADC 26` is not an ADC pin on
  RP2350B (ADC is GPIO40–47); the SPI-slave register contract in `solardeck_regs.h` has no
  implementation and competes with an incompatible UART/CRC-8 frame in `telemetryd` and an I²C
  transport in MVP/HW spec. Zen Reader cuts the shared 5 V rail that also feeds the IT8951.
- **H13 Back-powering.** WAKE, PWRFAIL and UART connect the always-on RP2350 straight to an
  unpowered CM4 (abs max VREF + 0.5 V); UART TX idles high.
- **H14 "Driverless UVC monitor sink"** — UVC is the camera class; the device would be a webcam. The
  real path is a CDC-NCM gadget plus a host screen-share app, at ≥0.5 s latency.

**RF, regulatory, mechanical, supply**
- **H15 No M.2 EG25-G exists** (LGA or Mini PCIe; Quectel's M.2 Cat-4 is EM05-G). EG25-G is 19 LTE
  bands, not "30+"; it misses B66/B71/B14/B48 used by US carriers. ST4SIM-200M is an M2M (SGP.02)
  eSIM — consumer QR activation via `lpac` (SGP.22) will not work.
- **H16 Antennas have nowhere to radiate**: full-face panel, full mu-metal sheet, 222×160 mm ground
  plane, midframe and possibly an aluminium shell. Reserve plastic windows and plan OTA tests early.
- **H17 Coexistence.** 240 mm free-space loss at 915 MHz is ~19 dB, not 25–35; SX1262 has no internal
  SAW; EU LoRa 863–870 MHz sits inside LTE B26 downlink. There is no region/power table in firmware,
  which AGENTS §4.4 requires before any TX config ships (the +22 dBm 868 MHz SOS beacon is
  non-compliant outside the 869.4–869.65 MHz high-power sub-band of ERC/REC 70-03 — sub-band limits
  UNVERIFIED, not re-fetched).
- **H18 REGULATORY.md corrections.** SAR evaluation is required, not "MPE may suffice" (LTE at
  23 dBm a few mm from the body is unlikely to meet the §1.1307(b)(3) exemption; current KDB 447498
  text not re-fetched — UNVERIFIED); PTCRB/GCF
  still certify the end device; a non-kit CM4 Wi-Fi antenna needs separate certification (CM4
  datasheet); EU battery passport/carbon label do not apply to a 32 Wh portable battery, but the
  user-replaceable-battery rule (Art. 11, from Feb 2027 — article and date UNVERIFIED) does. Missing rows: ISED, RED harmonised standards,
  **EN 18031** (RED cybersecurity, in force since Aug 2025), UKCA, FCC 15.225 (NFC).
- **H19 IP67 is untagged and ~24 apertures lack a sealing part**, including the 3.5 mm jack, ALPS
  rocker ("no washing"), open insert holes through a 2 mm floor, and an ePTFE vent (which also defeats
  the "<10% RH desiccant" claim). "Tested to 1 m" was never tested.
- **H20 Thermal.** CM4 under load is ~7 W (1.4 A at 5 V); the spec assumes 1.5 W. A PA12 shell cannot
  spread it; the back-skin touch limit (IEC 62368-1, ~48 °C — UNVERIFIED) and the 45 °C charge
  cut-off will be hit. Enclosure material (PA12 vs 6061)
  is undecided, and RF, thermal, mass and sealing all hang on it.
- **H21 Mass.** 620 g has no model behind it; a bottom-up estimate is ~760–1,150 g.
- **H22 BOM cost.** Mono 10.3" panel + IT8951 board alone retails at ~$203; the BOM line for colour +
  Wacom is $95–120. CM5, modem, cell are similarly low. Realistic 1–5 unit cost ≈ $830–1,100.
- **H23 CM5 pivot is half-applied.** `spec_values.cm5` cites ADR-032, which does not exist (the log
  ends at ADR-030), names SoC "BCM2718" (it is BCM2712), and leaves README/AGENTS/BOM/DTS on CM4. The
  Seeed BOM orders CM4104000, a Lite module with no eMMC.

The structural problem: **no phase compares a repo number with an external source.** The cheapest
high-value additions, in order:
1. `charger.vreg_min_v <= battery.charge_limit_v` with the charger's range stored next to its PN and
   datasheet revision. It fails today, which is correct.
2. A resolver that maps every LCSC C-number to its MPN (cached, checked into `hardware/production/`).
3. A panel-outline key that names the E Ink P/N and drawing revision; Phase 5 refuses an outline
   without one.
4. Red-control self-tests for every BANNED/SUPERSEDED regex (each pattern must match a canonical
   example), so a dead regex cannot hide again.

## 8. Recommended path to a first prototype

What a hardware team would do next, in order, smallest spend first:
1. **Dev-kit bench (≈ $400–600).** A CM4 IO board, Waveshare 10.3" IT8951 HAT, Pico 2 (RP2350), a
   BQ25798 EVM and one real LFP pouch. Measure the four numbers every pillar hangs on: CM4 lowest
   RAM-retaining power, IT8951 full/partial update time at the real SPI clock, IT8951 standby power,
   and LFP charge termination with the protector in circuit. `docs/DEV_KIT_BENCH.md` is the place to
   record them. Each measurement promotes a `null`/`modelled` key to `measured <date>`.
2. **Decide D1–D7** from those numbers, recording each as an ADR.
3. **Capture a real schematic** in KiCad with library symbols and wires, starting with power (charger,
   protector, gauge, rails), then the RP2350 minimum system, then the CM4 connector. Run ERC in CI
   with a KiCad build that has `sch erc`, and make a missing ERC a failure, not a skip.
4. **Generate fab outputs only from the routed board** via `kicad-cli`; delete
   `export_production_zip.py`'s synthetic gerbers so a placeholder can never reach a fab.
5. **EVT-0: power board only.** Charge, protect, gauge and sleep current on a small board before the
   full carrier.
