display row at $95–$120). The headline says **$383.30 – $408.30**.

unchecked by definition.

## B2 — There is no power budget at all, so the endurance claims are unauditable (blocker)

No document contains a generation/consumption table. The endurance claims that do exist are:

| Claim | Location | Arithmetic | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0.15 W strip outruns idle ⇒ "infinite" desk life | `MVP_ARCHITECTURE.md:101`, `STANDOUT_FEATURES.md:135` | 0.15 W × 4.28 kWh/m²/day × 0.70 = **0.45 Wh/day** vs 4.5 mW × 24 = **0.108 Wh/day** | ✅ **Holds, 4.2× margin** |
| "80+ days" LoRa repeater at 12–15 mW | `README.md:62,115`, `HARDWARE_SPEC.md:105` | 32 Wh ÷ (0.012–0.015 W × 24 h) = **89–111 days** | ✅ **Holds** |

So the two headline endurance numbers are *correct*. The blocker is that nothing states the
assumptions (4.28 kWh/m²/day insolation, 70 % derate, idle wattage), so no reader can recheck them and
no reviewer can spot the day a panel or load changes. An undocumented correct number and a fabricated
correct number are indistinguishable in a spec.

Also unstated: **harvested energy charges the pack, not the rails** (TPS25750 PD path, TPS61088 for SMA).
That is fine, but it means "runs on sunlight" and "runs while charging on sunlight" are different
claims and only the first is architectural.

Fix: Phase 14 recomputes both claims from `spec_values` primitives and fails if a claim drifts, and
`spec_values.power` records insolation, derate, idle and repeater loads.

## D3 — No regulatory or certification dimension exists anywhere (blocker for shipping)

There is no RF certification matrix, no safety-file list, no battery transport document, no import
classification. For a device with **cellular, Wi-Fi/BT, LoRa, and a user-swappable LiFePO4 pack** this is
the difference between a prototype and a product:

| Domain | Needed | Missing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cellular | PTCRB / GCF, carrier approval (EG25-G is pre-certified — must cite the module cert) | ✗ |
| Wi-Fi/BT | Module cert reuse (CM4 has it) vs intentional-radiator rules for the antenna | ✗ |
| LoRa / sub-GHz | FCC Part 15.247/15.231 + EU EN 300 220, per-region EIRP tables | ✗ |
| EMC / safety | FCC Part 15B, EN 55032, EN 62368-1 | ✗ |
| RF exposure | SAR/MPE assessment for the modem antenna 8 mm from the hand | ✗ |
| Battery | UN 38.3 (transport), IEC 62133-2, EU 2023/1542 battery passport | ✗ |
| Materials | RoHS/REACH, WEEE registration, Prop 65 | ✗ |

Add `docs/REGULATORY.md` with an owner and a target date per row, and make "no cert = no ship" explicit.

## D4 — Other findings (each re-verified against the file that owns it)

1. **ADR-022 was decided but never applied.** `docs/90-history/decisions.md` ADR-022 fixes gasket
   compression at **27 %** and torque at **0.45 N·m**; `HARDWARE_SPEC.md` §4 still said "compressed 30 %"
   and an older §7 draft line still said 0.25 N·m. A presence-only checker cannot see a leftover, so
   Phase 13 passed. **Fixed**, and Phase 13 now asserts superseded values are *absent* (that check
   immediately found 6 more leftovers: 4× "TMUX1575 8-channel" phrasing from ADR-004, now corrected to
   "2× TMUX1575, 4 channels each").
2. **No thermal model.** The only thermal claim is a converter junction temperature (§2.19). There is no
   enclosure thermal resistance, no ΔT budget for a sealed 620 g slab at 4.5 W, and no statement of
   whether the cell sits in the heat path — which is where calendar fade is decided.
3. **No firmware interface contract.** `firmware/rp2350/` exists but no RP2350↔CM4 register map does, so
   the two sides cannot be built or tested independently.
4. **No field-return path.** No RMA flow, no published spare-parts P/N list, no service SLA. For a
   30-year claim the repair path *is* the product.
5. **Ergonomics is unstated.** 620 g vs reMarkable 403 g / Boox Tab Ultra 480 g. Fine two-handed,
   not fine one-handed. The specs never choose.
6. **Salt fog vs exposed pogo pins.** G9 (ASTM B117, 96 h) plus bare gold pogo contacts needs a stated
   Au/Ni thickness and a bezel wipe spec; neither exists.

Also corrected in this audit's first draft: I asserted a "service hatch contradicts the adhesive rear"
contradiction. There is no adhesive-backed rear — §4.6 specifies a 12-screw gasketed seam. Dropped.

## 7. What the three audits together say

| Audit | Question | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Is the documentation true? | No — 10 blockers, now fixed |
| 2 | Will it survive 30 years? | Materials layer, no — 6 gaps, now specified |
| 3 | Does it add up, and can it ship? | **No — the money doesn't add up and there is no certification path** |

The pattern is consistent and diagnostic: every audit finds that the *documentation is internally
coherent and externally unverified*. Coherence is the thing this repo is best at; it is also the thing
that most easily disguises a wrong assumption, because a checker written against the same assumption
agrees with it.

The rule this earns: **every number needs one derivation, not two agreements.** Table sums, energy
budgets, and booleans like "is it certified" all belong in code that recomputes from primitives, never
in a second prose location.
